## Localizations

### English (U.S.): the primary language

- **Name** (28): `Bible Story Pals: Kids Bible`
- **Subtitle** (28): `Bilingual Bible stories, 3–6`
- **Promotional text** (148): `Twelve Bible stories read aloud in English and Spanish, each ending in a gentle mini-game. No ads, no accounts, works offline. Made for ages 3 to 6.`
- **Keywords** (95): `kids bible,bible stories,preschool,toddler,bilingual,spanish,christian,noah,sunday school,faith`

**Description:**

> Bible Story Pals brings twelve favorite Bible stories to life for little ones, read aloud in English, Spanish, or both.
>
> Every page is narrated, so children don't need to read. Each story ends with a gentle mini-game that practices early-learning skills. Nobody can lose: a wrong tap just wiggles.
>
> STORIES
> • God Made the World: tap sparkles to make the sun, trees and animals
> • Noah's Big Boat: bring the animals in two by two
> • Baby Moses: steer the basket down the river
> • David the Brave: pick five smooth stones
> • God Is My Shepherd (Psalm 23)
> • Jonah and the Big Fish: pop the colored bubbles
> • Daniel and the Lions: put the lions to sleep
> • Baby Jesus Is Born: light the stars 1 to 5
> • Jesus Is Baptized: a memory match about the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit
> • Jesus Calms the Storm, Jesus Feeds Everyone, and The Lost Sheep
>
> MADE FOR FAMILIES
> • English, Español, or EN + ES, which reads every page in both languages. Great for bilingual homes and dual-language classrooms.
> • Counting, colors, numbers, memory and new words in two languages
> • Gold stars to collect on the island map
> • Works offline, even on a plane or in the car
> • A grown-ups area behind a parent gate, with Bible references for each story and tips for talking together
>
> SAFE BY DESIGN
> No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No tracking. Nothing your child does leaves the device.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes")
> Bible Story Pals is a free, offline Bible story game for ages 3–6, in English and Spanish. There is no sign-in, no network use, no ads, no purchases and no data collection. Grown-ups area: tap "For grown-ups", then press and hold "Hold" for 3 seconds. Narration uses recorded clips bundled with the app, plus the iOS text-to-speech voice for lines that have no recording yet.

Sign-in required: **No** (leave the demo account empty).
